# Identity

Include identity proofing and impersonation; verify that identity cannot be obtained or retained without the required assurance.

Review:

identity creation; authentication; federation; credential lifecycle; recovery; session creation; session refresh; logout; revocation; re-authentication; privileged identity.

Questions:

How is identity established?; What proves the identity?; Can identity claims be forged or replayed?; Can recovery bypass the primary authentication strength?; Are sessions revocable?; Does privilege elevation require stronger assurance?; Can one identity create unlimited authentication attempts?.
